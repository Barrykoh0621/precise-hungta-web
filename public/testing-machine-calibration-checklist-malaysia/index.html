Calibration checklist Malaysia

Testing Machine Calibration Checklist for Factories and Labs

A practical checklist for Malaysia QA teams preparing tensile machines, universal testing machines, compression systems and load cells for calibration, service review or customer audit.

Ask about calibration support WhatsApp Hung Ta

Step 1

Confirm the machine identity

Basic details

Record the machine brand, model, serial number, year, maximum capacity and factory location before asking for support.

Load system

Prepare load cell details, capacity range, controller model and any recent replacement history if available.

Software and report

Note the software version, report format, graph output and whether the system exports PDF, Excel or customer-specific reports.

Step 2

Check condition before calibration

Mechanical condition

Check grips, fixtures, crosshead movement, oil leakage, abnormal sound, frame condition and safety guards.

Reading stability

Tell the service team if force readings drift, fail to return to zero, fluctuate or do not match previous results.

Known problems

List broken grips, software issues, sensor faults, power problems, emergency stop issues or failed reports before scheduling.

Step 3

Prepare records for audit readiness

Last calibration record

Keep the previous calibration date, certificate, service report and any repair records ready for review.

Audit deadline

Share the customer audit date or internal due date early. Waiting until audit week creates unnecessary risk.

Scope wording

Do not claim accreditation, standard coverage or measurement scope unless the current documents clearly support it.
