Common service situations

When to arrange a service review

Unstable readings

Force readings drift, fluctuate, fail to return to zero or do not match previous records.

Mechanical issues

Crosshead movement, hydraulic behavior, frame noise, grip slip or fixture damage needs checking before testing continues.

Software and report issues

Old software, missing reports, export problems or graph/report mismatch can slow down audit preparation.

Service and calibration

Service review before calibration can reduce audit risk

Calibration checks measurement performance. If the machine has mechanical, electronic, software or load cell problems, those issues may need service attention first. This is especially important for factories preparing customer audits or internal quality reviews.

Tensile systems

Check grips, extensometer needs, force range, crosshead control and report output before calibration planning.

Compression systems

Check platen condition, alignment, hydraulic response, load range and safety condition.

Universal systems

Confirm which tests are actually used: tensile, compression, bending, peel, shear or custom fixtures.
