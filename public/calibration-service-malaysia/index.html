Testing machine calibration questions

How often should a tensile testing machine be calibrated?

Use the interval required by your quality system, customer requirements and applicable test method. Usage, previous results and repairs can affect the review. Share your existing due date; there is no single interval suitable for every machine.

Can calibration fix unstable readings or a damaged machine?

Calibration evaluates measurement performance; it does not replace repair. Report drifting force readings, damaged grips, controller problems or recent load cell replacement so service needs can be reviewed first.

What affects the calibration support quotation?

The machine model, measurement range, number of load systems, condition, factory location and required records affect the enquiry. Send these details for a scope review rather than relying on a general price.
