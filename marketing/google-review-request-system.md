# Google Review Request System

Owner: Barry / Rachel
Business: Hung Ta Instrument
Status: Ready to use after Barry confirms the correct Google review link

## Purpose

Legitimate Google reviews improve trust and local SEO.

Do not ask for fake reviews.
Do not offer payment for reviews.
Do not write a review for the customer.

## Who To Ask First

Priority order:

1. Existing customers who bought machines and had a smooth experience.
2. Customers who received service or calibration-related support.
3. Long-term suppliers or partners who can honestly confirm the business relationship.
4. Repeat customers who know Hung Ta's technical support.

Avoid asking unhappy customers until the issue is handled.
