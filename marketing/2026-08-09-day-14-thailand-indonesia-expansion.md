## Recommended Ads Move

Do not add Thailand and Indonesia into the current Malaysia campaign.

Better structure:

- Keep current Malaysia campaign running.
- Create a separate `SEA - Thailand Indonesia Testing Machine` Search campaign.
- Start small at RM10/day to RM15/day total.
- Target Thailand and Indonesia only.
- Use English buyer keywords first.
- Send traffic to the new Thailand / Indonesia landing pages.

Reason: mixing countries inside one campaign makes the search-term data messy and can waste budget before we know which country has real buyer intent.
