## Landing Page Work

The `/products` page should become the main product landing page for ads. Each high-value product category should eventually get its own SEO page:

- `/products?category=utm` for universal testing machines
- `/products?category=rubber` for rubber and polymer testing
- `/products?category=wires` for wire and cable testing
- `/services` for calibration, repair and software support

## First 30 Days

1. Publish the updated website.
2. Submit `sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console.
3. Create Google Ads search campaigns for the keywords above.
4. Add WhatsApp and quote enquiry tracking.
5. Post 2 product posts per week on LinkedIn and Facebook.
6. Create one article per week answering buyer questions.

## Enquiry Qualification Questions

- What material or product do you need to test?
- What test standard do you follow?
- What capacity or force range do you need?
- Do you need tensile, compression, bending, peeling or fatigue testing?
- Do you need software reports, Excel/PDF export or calibration certificate?
