# Hung Ta Marketing Budget & Launch Plan

## Domain Budget

Recommended domains:
- hungtainstrument.com
- hungtainstrument.com.my

Estimated yearly cost:
- .com: about USD 10.98/year at Namecheap before renewal differences, roughly RM55-RM90/year depending exchange rate and registrar.
- .com.my: RM60/year through SSM/MYNIC special initiative or RM80/year usual price.

Estimated total for both:
RM115-RM170/year, plus possible small payment gateway or registrar fees.

Recommendation:
Buy both if available. Use hungtainstrument.com as the main website and point hungtainstrument.com.my to the same website.

## SEO Budget

Tools:
- Google Business Profile: free
- Google Search Console: free
- Google Analytics: free
- Sitemap submission: free

Recommended external agency budget:
RM0 for the first 3-6 months. Do the foundation internally first.

What to do first:
1. Complete website content.
2. Buy and connect domain.
3. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console.
4. Update Google Business Profile.
5. Build targeted SEO landing pages.
6. Review search data monthly.

## Google Ads Budget

Do not start with display/banner/app ads.

Recommended ad type:
Google Search Ads only.

Reason:
Hung Ta is a B2B testing machine supplier. Ads should appear when buyers actively search for testing equipment, not when people are playing games or reading general content.

Starter budget:
- Conservative: RM20/day, about RM600/month.
- Recommended test: RM50/day, about RM1,500/month.
- More aggressive: RM100/day, about RM3,000/month.

Recommended start:
RM600-RM1,500/month for the first 30-60 days.
